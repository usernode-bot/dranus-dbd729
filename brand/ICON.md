# DRANUS icon

A white house silhouette whose window glows like a movie screen, with a dark
indigo play triangle inside. Background: diagonal gradient, top left to bottom
right, `#2B1B7A` (deep indigo) through `#8A3B8F` to `#FF7A2F` (sunset orange),
with a faint white highlight at the top left. No text.

Source: `public/logo.svg` (rounded square, used for the favicon and the app
header). `brand/icon.png` is the same drawing rendered at 512 x 512 with
square corners, because the Homeroom tile rounds and crops it itself.
